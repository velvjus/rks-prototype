# RakanSales Roadshow Video Script

## Purpose

Create a simple, friendly, roadshow-ready video that helps passersby quickly understand what RakanSales does and encourages them to talk to the sales team.

## Target Audience

- Business owners
- Corporate sales teams
- Large companies managing high volumes of customer conversations
- Entrepreneurs who need a simpler way to manage daily sales conversations

## Core Positioning

RakanSales is not just a basic CRM. It is an AI-powered sales automation CRM that helps teams manage conversations, follow-ups, visibility, and sales actions in one place.

## Key Message

Not just a CRM. A complete AI-powered sales workflow, from conversation to next action.

## CTA

Transform your sales workflow today.

Secondary roadshow prompt:

Talk to our team.

## 60-Second Script

| Time | Voiceover | On-Screen Text | Visual Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-5s | Every business wants more leads. | More leads. More conversations. | Fast montage of leads arriving from WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, email, website chat, and webforms. |
| 5-10s | But when messages come from everywhere, things can get messy. | Too many channels? | Multiple inboxes, unread messages, scattered conversations, and a busy sales team trying to keep up. |
| 10-16s | Important customers get missed. Follow-ups are forgotten. And managers lose visibility. | Missed leads. Messy follow-ups. No clear visibility. | Show pending replies, overdue follow-ups, and a manager looking at scattered reports or disconnected tools. |
| 16-23s | RakanSales brings your sales conversations into one AI-powered workspace. | One AI-Powered Sales Automation CRM | Clean omnichannel inbox showing WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, email, website chat, and webforms in one view. |
| 23-31s | Your team can reply faster, tag conversations, filter priorities, and see what needs attention. | Reply faster. Prioritize better. | Simplified inbox with clear tags such as New Lead, Follow Up, Urgent, Replied, and Pending. Highlight work queue or priority sorting. |

## Silent Playback Notes

The video should still make sense without audio. Use big, clear text and avoid small CRM details that are hard to read from a distance.

Suggested silent text sequence:

1. More leads. More conversations.
2. Too many channels?
3. Missed leads. Messy follow-ups.
4. One AI-Powered Sales Automation CRM.
5. Reply faster. Prioritize better.
6. AI that supports your sales team.
7. From chat to next action.
8. Track performance. See what matters.
9. Transform your sales workflow today.

## Visual Style Direction

- Use simplified UI inspired by the real RakanSales system.
- Keep interface labels large and readable for roadshow viewing.
- Show real channels clearly: WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, email, website chat, and webforms.
- Avoid showing SMS for now.
- Use AI as a support layer, not as a replacement for the sales team.
- Make the dashboard feel useful for both business owners and managers.
- Keep the pacing quick, clean, and friendly.

## Production Notes

- Recommended length: about 60 seconds.
- Tone: simple, friendly, confident, and practical.
- Primary audience: business owners and corporate sales teams.
- Main roadshow objective: get viewers to talk to the sales team, then move toward demo booking or sign-up.
- Differentiator to emphasize: RakanSales manages more than stored leads. It helps teams move from conversation to follow-up, visibility, and the next sales action.
